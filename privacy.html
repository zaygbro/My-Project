Privacy

Draft policy · last updated August 2026
This is a pre-launch draft, not a final legal document. Francisity is currently in private beta with no billing or public accounts live yet. This page states our intended policy so you know what to expect — it will be reviewed by counsel and re-published before general availability.

What we collect

When you use Francisity, we collect what's needed to draft and host your site:

  • Your brief text, reference links, and any images or moodboards you provide
  • The site content the engines draft — copy, layout, and assets
  • Your account email and billing contact, once accounts and billing exist
  • Basic technical logs (request timestamps, error traces) needed to operate the service

How it's used

Your brief and site content are used to generate, host, and let you edit your site. We do not sell your content or brief to third parties. Our intent is that briefs and site content are not used to train general-purpose AI models beyond generating your own site, unless you explicitly opt in to share a build for our public showcase.

Where it's stored

Site content and account data are stored with infrastructure providers selected for security and reliability. We'll name specific subprocessors and regions here once that infrastructure is finalized for general availability.

Deletion

You can delete a site and its underlying content at any time from your dashboard once accounts ship. Deleting a site removes its draft history and hosted content; account deletion removes your billing and contact information, subject to what we're legally required to retain (e.g. invoices).

Cookies & analytics

The marketing site uses minimal, privacy-respecting analytics to understand traffic — no third-party ad trackers. Published sites will have their own analytics, opt-in and disclosed separately.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a request to delete your data: reach us through the contact link in the footer once support channels are live.
